bili-player-bridge.js Protocol Tests

bili-player-bridge.js is a dynamic content script (chrome.scripting.registerContentScripts, armed by md-video.js) injected into every frame of https://player.bilibili.com/* once the reader has granted that origin. Its own header comment says it stays "inert until greeted": nothing is read from the page and nothing is posted until a hello arrives from window.parent at the extension's own origin. This suite locks the cheapest, most valuable slice of that invariant.
