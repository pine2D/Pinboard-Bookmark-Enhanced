Offline English–Chinese dictionary

Adds Chinese definitions above the English entry when you look up an English word. The English entry still loads as usual.

Expects a CSV file whose first line is the column header and includes word, translation, tag, collins, oxford, frq and bnc. Prebuilt dictionary packages such as mdx, eudic, stardict, mobi and sqlite are different formats and cannot be imported. This project does not provide, recommend or verify a source for the data, and is not affiliated with it. You supply the file and are responsible for its licence.

Wayback Machine Archiving

After each save, ask the Internet Archive to snapshot the page (best effort, never blocks saving). Sends the saved URL to archive.org; off by default.

Batch saves can fire many archive requests at once; anonymous quota is limited.

With a free archive.org account key, archiving uses the authenticated API: higher quota, server-side dedup, reliable results.

Theme

Switches the popup, this settings page, the notes & vocabulary page and the reader between light and dark. Presets with a built-in scheme (Dracula, for example) override it on the pages that follow the preset; the reader can override it per device from its "Aa" panel.

When on, the popup, this settings page and the notes & vocabulary page use the palette of the selected Pinboard preset; when off they use the default palette in the light/dark mode above. The reader always keeps its own reading palette.
