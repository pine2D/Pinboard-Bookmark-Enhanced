## English (en)

**Extension name:** Bookmark Enhanced

```text
⚠ Choose one bookmark service in Settings. Karakeep and linkding work without a Pinboard account. Pinboard is an independent paid service and requires your own account and API token. This third-party extension is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Pinboard. AI features are optional and use an API key you supply for the LLM provider of your choice.

# Save
- Save to the service you choose: Pinboard, Karakeep or linkding, with the title, description and selected text filled in.
- AI tags and summaries: suggested from the article itself, using your own API key.
- Never lose a save: saves made offline wait in a queue and go through once you reconnect.

# Read
- A clean reader for any page: no ads or sidebars, with five-color highlights and notes.
- Read in two languages: translate the whole page and keep the original beside it.
- Ask the page: answers cite the article and jump to the quoted sentence.
- Look up words in place: definitions and pronunciation without leaving the page; send saved words to Anki.
- Watch while you read: YouTube and bilibili videos play beside a transcript that follows along.

# Keep
- One library for everything you kept: highlights, notes, saved words and past questions, synced through your own Google Drive.
- Send or download: send articles to your notes app or your own webhook, or save them as e-books for your e-reader.

# Make Pinboard yours
- 13 themes for pinboard.in: light and dark looks for the site itself, plus room for your own custom CSS.
- Tidy your tags: merge near-duplicates and prune the ones you rarely use.
- Auto-archive to the Wayback Machine: pages you save stay readable after the original link dies.
```
